# Enterprise Travel & Expense (T&E) Policy (v2.4)

## Section 1: Meals and Daily Per Diem

### SEC-1.1: Daily Per Diem Limit
- Individual business travel meals are subject to a strict **$75.00 daily per diem cap** per calendar day.
- This daily cap covers all individual meals consumed in a single day (breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks, and non-alcoholic beverages).
- Suggested single meal guidelines: Breakfast ($15.00), Lunch ($25.00), Dinner ($35.00). However, compliance is audited strictly against the **$75.00 total daily meal limit**.
- Any cumulative meal expenditure exceeding $75.00 on a single day is **non-reimbursable** for the excess amount.
- Consecutive receipts on the same calendar day must be combined when assessing the $75.00 limit.

### SEC-1.2: Alcohol Policy
- Alcohol is only eligible for reimbursement when consumed during evening dinner or approved client entertainment events. Alcohol consumed during solo breakfast or solo lunch is **strictly non-reimbursable (100% disallowed)**.
- For eligible dinners, total alcohol charges (including beer, wine, cocktails, and liquor) must **not exceed 20% of the meal subtotal** (pre-tax, pre-tip).
- Any alcohol expenditure exceeding the 20% cap is disallowable and will be deducted from the reimbursement total.
- Employees must submit itemized receipts clearly delineating food items from alcoholic beverages. If drinks are labeled ambiguously (e.g., "beverage", "refreshment") but verified as alcoholic, the 20% cap strictly applies.

### SEC-1.3: Itemized Receipt Documentation
- An **itemized receipt** showing detailed individual line items, quantities, and prices is mandatory for all meal expenses exceeding **$25.00**.
- Credit card charge slips showing only the total amount without itemization are **not acceptable documentation** for claims over $25.00 and will be flagged for review until itemized proof is supplied.
- Gratuity (tip) is reimbursable up to a maximum of **20%** of the pre-tax bill.

## Section 2: Ground Transportation & Rideshares

### SEC-2.1: Public Transit Preference & Rideshare Limitation
- Employees are expected to utilize public transit (subway, metro, light rail, commuter train, or bus) for ground transportation whenever practical.
- Rideshare services (e.g., Uber, Lyft, taxi, private car) are **strictly disallowed** if public transit transit time between origin and destination is **under 15 minutes**.
- If public transit is under 15 minutes and rideshare is used without a documented approved exception, the entire rideshare fare is **non-reimbursable**.

### SEC-2.2: Approved Ground Transit Exceptions
Rideshare is permitted even when public transit is under 15 minutes ONLY under the following documented conditions:
1. **Late-Night Travel**: Travel occurring between **10:00 PM (22:00) and 6:00 AM (06:00)** for personal safety.
2. **Heavy Equipment / Materials**: Employee is transporting bulky business materials, tradeshow booth fixtures, or equipment exceeding 30 lbs.
3. **Severe Weather / Safety Hazards**: Extreme weather advisories or documented transit outages that render public transit unsafe or inoperable.
- The employee must provide an explicit justification note referencing the exception upon expense submission.

## Section 3: Client Entertainment & Hospitality

### SEC-3.1: Documentation & Attendee Requirements
- Client entertainment expenses (meals, hosting, approved events) require full documentation:
  1. **Full names, professional titles, and company affiliations** of all attendees (both internal employees and external clients/partners).
  2. A specific, substantive **business justification** articulating the commercial purpose of the meeting and anticipated business outcome. Vague justifications such as "business dinner" or "met with client" are insufficient.
- Submissions missing attendee details or clear justification will be flagged for review (`MISSING_INFO`) and held until amended.

### SEC-3.2: Per-Person Spending Cap
- Client entertainment meals are subject to a maximum cap of **$150.00 per attendee** (inclusive of food, drinks, tax, and tip).
- Spending exceeding $150.00 per person will have the excess amount disallowed.

## Section 4: Lodging & Incidentals

### SEC-4.1: Lodging Rate Limits
- Hotel accommodations must be booked at standard single-room corporate rates.
- Maximum allowable room rates:
  - Tier 1 Cities (New York, San Francisco, London, Tokyo): **$300.00 / night** (pre-tax).
  - All Other Cities: **$200.00 / night** (pre-tax).

### SEC-4.2: Non-Reimbursable Personal Incidentals
- Personal incidentals charged to hotel folios are strictly non-reimbursable and must be removed prior to submission.
- Prohibited items include: in-room minibar snacks/drinks, pay-per-view movies, spa/gym personal treatments, laundry for trips under 4 days, and dry cleaning.

---

## Section 5: Anti-Fraud & Expense Integrity

### SEC-5.1: Artificial Receipt Splitting
- Intentionally splitting a single transaction into multiple smaller receipts (e.g., requesting two separate bills of $45 and $55 at the same venue to bypass the $75 daily cap or receipt itemization threshold) is considered a policy violation.
- Audits must aggregate all receipts from the same vendor or within the same meal timeframe.

### SEC-5.2: Trip Date & Route Alignment
- Expense dates must match authorized business travel dates.
- Transportation receipts must align with documented business destinations and itineraries.
